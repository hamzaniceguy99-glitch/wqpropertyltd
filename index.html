How we work

A UK company, trading since 2017

WQ Property Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 10749611, incorporated in May 2017, with its registered office at 3rd Floor, 16 Black Friars Lane, London EC4V 6EB.

We are not solicitors, surveyors or accountants, and we give no legal, valuation or tax advice — those professionals are appointed separately on every project. Where we manage property or handle client money, funds are held in a client account and we belong to a government-approved redress scheme with client money protection in place.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you develop your own sites or work for clients?

Both. Some projects are on our own account and others are run for clients under a written appointment, with the basis agreed before anything starts.

Can you guarantee planning permission?

No, and nobody can. We assess the policy position and precedent honestly and tell you where the risk sits before you commit.

Do you hold client money?

Where we manage property, yes, in a client account with client money protection in place and membership of a government-approved redress scheme.

Are you surveyors or architects?

No. We appoint and coordinate them. Keeping those roles separate is how a client gets independent advice rather than one firm marking its own work.

What size of project do you take on?

Tell us the site and we'll be straightforward about whether it suits us. We'd rather decline than stretch.

How are fees structured?

Set out in the written appointment before work begins — fixed, staged or percentage depending on the project.

Got a site or a property to discuss?

A straightforward conversation and an honest view, with no obligation.
